# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own the quality strategy, test planning, and acceptance validation for projects. They collaborate with developers and product managers to ensure features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards before release.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain test plans aligned with project scope and milestones
- Design and execute manual and automated test cases
- Coordinate acceptance testing with product and stakeholders
- Track and triage defects, prioritizing based on severity and impact
- Recommend quality improvements and test automation opportunities
- Validate releases in staging and production environments

### Goals
- Deliver high-quality software that meets or exceeds customer expectations
- Reduce defects and rework through early detection
- Improve test coverage and automation to reduce manual effort

### Typical Communication
- Weekly QA sync with development and product teams
- Test plan reviews during planning phase
- Defect reports and triage meetings
- Release readiness sign-offs

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide technical direction, evaluate architectural trade-offs, and ensure technical decisions align with long-term product vision and scalability goals. They mentor developers and champion code quality.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical standards and architectural patterns
- Review and approve major design decisions
- Mentor junior developers and conduct technical design reviews
- Identify technical debt and propose mitigation strategies
- Collaborate with product and project managers on technical feasibility
- Champion best practices for testing, security, and observability

### Goals
- Build scalable, maintainable systems that support future growth
- Elevate engineering practices and team capability
- Reduce technical risk and production incidents

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code review feedback and mentoring sessions
- Technical roadmap alignment with product leadership
- Incident retrospectives and post-mortems

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile processes, remove impediments, and coach teams on agile best practices. They enable teams to work efficiently and continuously improve their delivery cadence.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, daily standups, and retrospectives
- Identify and help resolve blockers and dependencies
- Coach the team on agile principles and ceremonies
- Track velocity and team health metrics
- Work with Project Managers to optimize delivery workflows
- Foster a culture of continuous improvement and psychological safety

### Goals
- Increase team productivity and delivery predictability
- Improve team collaboration and communication
- Build sustainable, high-performing delivery teams

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- One-on-ones with team members to identify blockers
- Retrospective facilitation and action item tracking
- Coaching conversations on process improvements

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors represent business, customer, or strategic interests. They define priorities, approve major scope changes, allocate resources, and ensure project outcomes align with organizational goals.

### Responsibilities
- Communicate business requirements and success metrics
- Approve scope changes and trade-offs
- Allocate budget and resource commitments
- Provide feedback on deliverables and user experience
- Escalate blockers and risks that impact organizational goals
- Share project outcomes with broader leadership

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver maximum business value
- Maintain alignment between delivery and strategic objectives
- Remove organizational barriers to success

### Typical Communication
- Monthly or milestone-based status updates
- Executive steering committee meetings
- Approval gates at key decision points
- Ad-hoc escalations for critical issues

---

## DevOps / Release Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps and Release Engineers manage deployment pipelines, infrastructure, and production monitoring. They ensure reliable, secure, and observable systems that support rapid, safe releases.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain CI/CD pipelines and infrastructure
- Automate deployment and rollback processes
- Configure monitoring, logging, and alerting for production systems
- Collaborate with developers on deployment strategies and observability
- Manage infrastructure-as-code and configuration management
- Support incident response and production troubleshooting

### Goals
- Enable fast, reliable, and safe deployments
- Maintain high system availability and performance
- Reduce deployment risk through automation and observability

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and deployment coordination
- Infrastructure and deployment pipeline reviews
- Incident response and post-mortems
- Observability and monitoring discussions
